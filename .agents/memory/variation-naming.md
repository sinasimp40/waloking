---
name: Variation naming
description: Distinguish a game's overall title from individual edition names in the variation picker.
---

For games with variations, use the overall main title in both the featured
banner and picker heading, separate from selectable edition names.

**Why:** The user wants a heading such as “Rules of Survival” rather than repeating
the edition-specific “Rules of Survival : Legacy” above the edition choices.

**How to apply:** Keep the heading separately configurable. Do not automatically
strip subtitles or rename a customer's existing executable choices. When changing
this title behavior, check both the banner and picker; fixing only the picker
previously left the user's featured banner showing the first edition's name.

Picker choices should use their own configured artwork and edition names, not
play symbols or “Base game” / “Variation” badges.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested individual icons and removal of these
labels from the version picker.

**How to apply:** Provide an icon field for each variation and preserve old
records without icons using a neutral fallback rather than a play symbol.
